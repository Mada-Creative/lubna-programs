## Art Direction Guidelines Followed

1. **No Clichés**: Avoided staged high-fives, dramatic crying closeups, needles/drugs/alcohol visual representations, and corporate handshake stock imagery.
2. **Editorial Feel**: Sourced candid, natural-light documentary-style photography that represents real learning, collaboration, and supportive environments.
3. **Format**: All files downloaded natively as high-efficiency WebP files (`&fm=webp`) at balanced viewport widths (`w=600`) to prevent high network payloads and maintain layout stability.
